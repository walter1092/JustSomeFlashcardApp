3. RULES FOR THE 3 CARD TYPES:

A. CONCEPT CARDS (`type: "concept"`):
- `Term`: Name of the theorem, principle, or formula (LaTeX `$...$` supported).
- `Explanation`: Clear, rigorous definition or formula (`$...$` inline or `$$...$$` block LaTeX).
- `Examples` (optional): Brief worked application or boundary case.
- `Notes` (optional): Common exam traps, sign conventions, or red/green ink mistake reminders.
- `Optional`: Set to `true` ONLY if the slide/topic is explicitly non-examinable/optional.

B. QUIZ / MCQ CARDS (`type: "quiz"`):
- `Question`: Clear problem statement or conceptual question (use `___` for blanks if needed).
- `Options`: 4 plausible options separated by semicolons (`;`).
- `Answer`: Must match the exact text of the correct option(s) (separate multiple correct options with `;`).
- `Explanation`: Step-by-step reasoning explaining why the answer is correct and why common trap options fail.

C. OPEN-ENDED CARDS (`type: "oeq"`):

If the answer is a complex mathematical expression, either turn the question into an MCQ or ask for a specific numerical value / plain-English term outside LaTeX (e.g., `**3|three**`).
  * For multi-case inequality proofs or pure LaTeX derivations where auto-marking does not apply, bolding is optional.
- `Hint` (optional but encouraged): Include formulas, constants, or standard identities that would normally be provided on an exam formula sheet (hidden behind the card's `?` hint button during study).
- `Note` (optional): Extra post-check explanation or derivation steps.
- `DraftNotes` (optional): Source slide/page reference (e.g., "Lecture 3, Slide 12 — Starred mistake").
